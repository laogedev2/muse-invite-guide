Who is Muse for? (And who should skip it)

Muse is Meta's personal AI agent — it has its own computer, browser and filesystem, connects to your email, calendar and accounts, keeps long-term memory, and runs scheduled background jobs. That's a different animal from a chatbot. This page is an honest fit check so you don't waste your time — or miss the invite bonus if it is for you.

✅ 5 signs Muse is right for you

FIT

You want jobs finished, not just answers

You'd rather say "triage my inbox and draft replies" or "research this and give me a sourced brief" and get back a done result — Muse works in the background and reports when it's complete.

FIT

You live in email, calendar and docs

Muse connects to your accounts and remembers your preferences across sessions, so recurring work ("weekly report", "trip plan") gets faster instead of starting from zero each time.

FIT

You want things to happen on a schedule

Price watches, flight tracking, daily briefings, reminders — Muse runs scheduled jobs while you're away. See 30 real use cases for the full list.

FIT

You produce deliverables

Documents, decks, spreadsheets, images, video, audio, working code — Muse builds the artifact, not just the outline.

FIT

You're in the US or Canada

Muse is currently available in the United States and Canada (per the official page). If that's you, you're eligible for the invite program.

🚫 3 signs you should skip it

SKIP

You're outside the service regions

If you're not in the US or Canada, you can't use Muse right now — and you can't redeem the invite code either. Check the official page for the latest availability before anything else.

SKIP

You just want quick chat answers

If 90% of what you need is "answer this question right now", a regular chatbot is lighter and faster. Muse's strength is doing multi-step work over time, not one-line Q&A.

SKIP

You won't redeem within 48 hours

The invite-code entry only exists during the first 48 hours after you join — then it disappears and the 1 billion token bonus is gone for both sides. If you can't do Settings → redeem in the next two days, wait until you can.
