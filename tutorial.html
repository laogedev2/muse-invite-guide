How to Join Muse & Redeem Invite Code BMIFSK

The whole thing takes about three minutes — but one detail matters more than everything else: the redemption entry only exists during the first 48 hours after you join. So do it in this order: join → redeem → explore. If you explore first and come back two days later, the entry is gone and the tokens are gone with it.

The code you will enter in Step 3
BMIFSK
Start at muse.ai/join →

1Join Muse

Go to muse.ai/join and complete the sign-up. You can join on the web, iOS, or Android. Finish the full registration (account created and signed in) — the 48-hour redemption clock starts when you join, so move straight to Step 2.

2Open the redemption entry in Settings

On the web, follow this path:

Settings → General → Usage → Redeem invite code

In the mobile app, follow this path:

Settings → Redeem token

Can't find it? Check that you're signed in to the account you just created, and remember the entry only appears within the first 48 hours after joining.

3Redeem the code BMIFSK

Type BMIFSK into the invite-code field (watch for typos — B-M-I-F-S-K, all caps) and confirm. You should see a success confirmation. If the code is rejected, re-check the spelling first; if the entry itself is missing, you are likely past the 48-hour window (see the FAQ).

4Tokens land for both sides

Once the redemption confirms, both you and the inviter receive 1 billion Muse tokens. Exact reward terms, validity and eligibility are as stated on the official invite page at muse.ai/join — that page governs if anything differs. Now you're free to explore: a good first move is to hand Muse three tasks from the 30 use cases list.

⏰ The 48-hour rule: the invite-code entry is only open during the first 48 hours after joining. After that, the entry disappears and this batch of tokens can no longer be claimed. Redeem first, explore after — not the other way around.

Availability note

Muse is currently available in the United States and Canada. Availability is determined by the official page — if your region isn't supported yet, the join page will tell you. Reward details and validity likewise follow the official invite page.

Troubleshooting

  • No "Redeem invite code" entry: most likely more than 48 hours have passed since you joined, or you're signed in to a different account than the one you just created.
  • Code rejected: re-type BMIFSK carefully — it's easy to mistype the middle letters. Make sure there are no leading/trailing spaces.
  • Web vs app paths differ: web is Settings → General → Usage → Redeem invite code; the app shortens it to Settings → Redeem token. Both lead to the same redemption.
  • Tokens not visible yet: re-check after the redemption confirmation, and consult the official invite page for the authoritative reward terms.
