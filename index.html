What is Muse?

Most AI assistants stop at advice. Muse is built to do: it runs scheduled jobs in the background, reads and triages your inbox, drafts and sends email with your approval, drives a real browser to run errands on websites, writes and executes code on its own machine, produces finished documents, decks, images, short videos and spoken audio, tracks flights and prices, plans trips end to end, and keeps long-term memory of your standing preferences. You can even delegate to it from WhatsApp. In short: you hand it busywork in plain words, and it hands back finished work.
